Moon: surface assets S0

Moon side: driven by energetic protons (SEP). There is no magnetic field, and even in Earth's magnetotail the Moon is not shielded from SEPs (Liuzzo et al., GRL 2023).

Earth: affected systems S0G0

Earth side: the same protons hit polar HF radio, satellites and crew dose. The CME's magnetic storm (G-scale) arrives later and hits GNSS and power grids. Impact rules follow the NOAA space weather scales (swpc.noaa.gov).

Why this matters for the pitch

One solar eruption, two timelines: protons reach the Moon and Earth within an hour, and the CME shock arrives 1–3 days later. A lunar operator and an Earth satellite operator need the same warning but take different actions. The "restart window" logic serves both.

1 · Ingest

DONKI (flares, CMEs, SEPs), NOAA SWPC GOES protons, SEP Scoreboard forecasts, LRO/CRaTER dose (ISWA HAPI)
→

2 · Fuse

Ensemble probability that S1/S2/S3 is exceeded in 6/24/48 h, with uncertainty. We don't build a new forecast model; we combine the community ones.
→

3 · Translate

Per-asset rule: shielding (g/cm²), part sensitivity, mission phase → CONTINUE / SAFE / SHUTDOWN
→

4 · Restart

All-clear only after flux stays below threshold for N hours and no second CME is on the way (hysteresis)
→

5 · Audit

Every alert logs its inputs. Backtest against historic events with SPHINX metrics (HSS, false alarms, warning time)

Endpoints (proposal)
GET  /v1/environment/now            current flux, S/G scale, sources
GET  /v1/forecast?horizon=6h,24h,48h  ensemble probabilities ± spread
POST /v1/assets                     register an asset profile
     { "shielding_gcm2": 2, "see_sensitivity": "high",
       "phase": "drilling", "location": [-84.79, 29.20] }
GET  /v1/assets/{id}/advisory       action + restart window
WS   /v1/stream                     push alerts (machine-to-machine)
GET  /v1/backtest?event=2024-05-11  replay + scores

      
Every response carries sources[] with the URLs and timestamps it used. That makes it auditable, which matches the team's explainability rule.
