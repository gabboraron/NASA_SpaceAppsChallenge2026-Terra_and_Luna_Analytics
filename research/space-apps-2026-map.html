Rules that shape our build hard limits in orange

  • DemoVideo ≤ 30 s or deck ≤ 7 slides (title slide counts). Exceeding either removes the project from global judging. Hosted publicly.
  • NASA dataAt least one NASA dataset or resource for any Global Award. Judges score whether it is integral, not decorative.
  • Final linkPublic project link, no login. A FortiGuard rating request is suggested for new domains so judges can open them.
  • LanguageEnglish everywhere. Spoken video in another language needs English subtitles.
  • TeamMax 6 members, one team per person, one challenge per team. No changes after the deadline.
  • TimingNo work on the challenge before the hackathon. Last save before the deadline is judged.
  • AI ("SI")Allowed for building, but the guide says SI should not be used to come up with the solution idea. Every submission discloses SI use (not shown to judges).
  • AI labelsVisible watermark on AI images and video. AI-made code, data and audio acknowledged in text and metadata. Copilot code counts.
  • LicenseOpen source (OSI). If none is given, NASA applies Apache-2.0. Third-party data must be free or OSI-licensed; no ITAR/EAR.
  • ContentNo promotion of commercial products or services, no PII, no likeness of minors, no NASA logos in generated media. Pitch a "B2B API" as a capability, not as a product for sale.
  • CitationsCite every source. Missing citations can cost a nomination or award.
  • EligibilityNo participation through an affiliation with China or a Chinese-owned entity. US federal civil servants cannot register.

Shortlist given 36 hours left

Option A · strongest fit

Slip Day + Lunar Shield layer

Challenge 11. Core: horizon from LOLA, Sun/Earth from Horizons, power and comms per site × day. Add Lunar Shield as one extra risk column: open-sky fraction from the same horizon + live NOAA/DONKI SEP status + a restart rule. Keeps the team's lunar idea and scores on Relevance.

Option B · clean fit

Cross-planetary Similarity Search

Challenge 9. Data-heavy, highly visual, uses GIS and geophysics skills. Must beat last year's similar winner with the "where the analogy breaks" view.

Option C · team skills

Research Navigator or SAR change

Challenges 2 or 5. Earth-observation route if the team prefers Benedek's and Yhair's strengths. Larger competitor pool.

Lunar Shield alone is a weak fit

No 2026 challenge asks for operational space-weather protection of lunar hardware. Submitted alone under challenge 11, it would lose Relevance points. It is strongest as one layer inside Option A. The deep-dive on Lunar Shield science, existing solutions and data is in the repo under research/.
