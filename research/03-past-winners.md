# Past Global Winners 2021–2025: what they tell us

Source: `hackathon/NASA Space Apps Global Winners 2021-2025 (CSV).csv` (50 winners, 10 awards × 5 years).

## Patterns
- **Format:** about half (23/50) are working web prototypes, apps or dashboards; 12 are ML or scientific analyses; 11 are
  concepts, creative pieces or hardware; 4 are games. A working, public web demo is the norm, not a bonus.
- **Decision support built on NASA data wins Data, Impact and Technology awards:** Waterwise and 2plant (2024, farm decision tools),
  SkySense (2025 Best Use of Technology: "safer activity time windows" from EO + AI, the same pattern as
  Lunar Shield's "when to stop, when to restart"), Astro Sweepers (2025 Galactic Impact: a risk index per space object).
- **Space weather has won repeatedly:**
  - 2022 Best Use of Science: "What's New?", an ML pipeline for solar-storm probability.
  - 2022 Art & Technology: "Earth, Wind & Flare", an **IoT hardware device** explaining space weather
    (precedent for the Lunar Shield desk-rover demo).
  - 2023 Best Use of Data: "Storm Prophet", LSTM geomagnetic-storm prediction from DSCOVR.
  - 2025 Best Mission Concept: PureFlow, a habitat design tool validated against **real space-weather threats**.
  - 2025 Best Storytelling: a children's space-weather story.
  → Judges like space weather, but "ML predicts solar storms" has already won, so novelty must come from
  the action layer (per-asset safe mode, restart window), not the forecast.
- **The Moon wins through data visualization:** Moonquake Map won 4 awards (2022 Galactic Impact + Local Impact; 2023 Science + Technology).
- **Games and stories win Inspirational, Storytelling and Art & Technology** (Trail to Mars 2021 radiation/crew game, Eco-Metropolis 2024,
  farm mini-games 2025). This is relevant if Lunar Shield becomes a Junior Astronaut game (ch. 3) or for the discarded-hardware story (ch. 1).
- **EO, agriculture and phenology** recur: BloomWatch/QUENARIS 2025 Local Impact (phenology + satellites), so the Pollen idea has
  a precedent but also competition.
- No winner in the CSV covers Earth analog sites or lunar landing-site planning. The "similar topic won last year" note in the kick-off doc
  may refer to a local or nominee-level award; check before relying on it.

## Implications for our choice
| Idea | Closest past winner(s) | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Lunar Shield (layer or standalone) | Storm Prophet 2023, What's New? 2022, SkySense 2025, PureFlow 2025 | Proven judge appetite. Differentiate on action + restart, not forecasting |
| Slip Day (ch. 11) | Landsat Connect 2024 (Best Mission Concept, planning tool), Moonquake maps | Mission-planning web tools win Best Mission Concept |
| Similarity Search (ch. 9) | none direct | Open territory, needs a strong visual |
| Trailbreaker (ch. 10) | Mohakash 2021 (planetary exploration concept) | Few Mars-route winners |
| Research Navigator (ch. 2) | Waterwise 2024, G.R.O.W. 2024 (GIS made accessible) | Crowded EO space, needs a sharp angle |
| Pollen / SAR (ch. 5/6) | QUENARIS 2025, 2plant 2024 | Precedent + competition |
| Hardware story (ch. 1) | Space Travelers 2021, HerCode 2025 (storytelling) | Only as a story or game for kids |

## Other resource checked
- https://spacestatsonline.com: a one-person launch-statistics site (launches per year, country, rocket, launch site).
  No API, no stated licence, no lunar, Mars, space-weather or hardware-location data. **Not useful for our ideas**
  except as background launch-count context.
